# Lappaa vs. Loom — Comparison

> Loom combines screen recording with cloud hosting and sharing via links for async workplace collaboration. Lappaa is a mobile-first, on-device audio and video creator designed for social media and direct messaging.

## Overview

| Feature | Lappaa | Loom |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Focus** | Social media, fast professional updates, messaging | Workplace async communication, screen walkthroughs |
| **Platforms** | Apple Ecosystem (iOS, iPadOS) | Multi-platform (Browser, Mac/PC, iOS, Android) |
| **Input Focus** | Camera + Microphone | Screen + Webcam + Microphone |
| **Processing** | Local (On-device) | Cloud-based storage and processing (with local recording) |
| **Pricing** | Free + Pro (Subscription or Lifetime) | Free tier + Paid monthly subscriptions |

---

## Output vs. Hosting

The fundamental difference between the two tools is how they handle the final video.

**Loom** acts as a hosting platform. When you finish recording, the video is uploaded to Loom's cloud servers. You share a link, and the recipient watches the video on Loom's website, where they can leave timestamped comments and reactions.

**Lappaa** acts as an originator tool. When you finish recording, it exports a standard video file directly to your device. You can then share this file natively on messaging apps (WhatsApp, Signal, iMessage) or social platforms (LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok). The recipient watches the video directly in their feed or chat, without clicking an external link.

---

## Workflow Focus

**Loom** is optimized for screen sharing and async workplace collaboration. If you need to demonstrate software, record a bug report, or walk through a presentation with your camera in a picture-in-picture bubble, Loom is built specifically for that workflow.

**Lappaa** is optimized for audio-first and "talking head" communication. If you are recording a voice note or a quick professional update, Lappaa automatically applies audio-reactive visualizers, AI-generated backgrounds, and fast-paced captions. 

---

## Captions and Accessibility

Both tools support automatic transcription, but they display captions differently.

In **Loom**, transcripts and closed captions (CC) are processed and displayed within the Loom web player, allowing viewers to search the text or toggle captions on and off.

In **Lappaa**, transcription happens locally on your device. Lappaa can embed (burn) the captions directly into the exported video file. This ensures the text is permanently visible and instantly readable on mute, regardless of which social platform or messaging app it is posted to.

---

## Privacy & Cloud Dependency

| Capability | Lappaa | Loom |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Processing Location** | On-device | Cloud servers |
| **Account Required**| No account required for core functionality | Typically required (Workspace/SSO) |
| **Offline Support** | Core features work offline | Recording possible offline; sharing and playback require internet |

Loom is designed for enterprise and team environments where cloud storage, single sign-on (SSO), and centralized workspaces are standard requirements. 

Lappaa is designed for individual creators and professionals who prefer local processing. Core features, including transcription and video generation, run entirely on your device without requiring a cloud upload or a user account.

---

## Summary

### Choose **Lappaa** if you:
- Need to post a captioned video file directly to social networks like LinkedIn, Instagram, or TikTok.
- Want to send visual voice notes via messaging apps (WhatsApp, iMessage) that recipients can read on mute.
- Are recording audio-only or "talking head" content away from a desk.
- Prefer local, on-device processing without cloud storage.

### Choose **Loom** if you:
- Need to record your screen to demonstrate software, documents, or slides.
- Need viewer analytics (knowing who watched your video and for how long).
- Are communicating internally with a team that uses timestamped comments and reactions.
- Prefer sharing URLs instead of uploading video files.
