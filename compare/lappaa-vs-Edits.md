Lappaa is a mobile-first, on-device audio and video creator designed to turn one-take recordings into captioned, platform-ready videos for social sharing and messaging.

## Workflow: Originator vs. Editor

Both apps allow you to export clean, platform-ready video files, but their core workflows serve different purposes.

**Instagram Edits** is a standalone editor built for short-form video creation. If you need to stitch multiple clips together, sync them to a trending audio track, apply AR filters, and meticulously craft visual transitions, Edits provides the traditional non-linear timeline required for that work.

**Lappaa** acts as an originator tool built for speed. Its workflow is designed around the "one-take" philosophy: you record your voice or video, apply a saved visual template (background, visualiser, and font), and export. It is designed for those who want to communicate a thought visually without spending time in a timeline editor.

---

## Audio Fidelity and Mastering

For musicians, podcasters, and professionals, audio quality is a primary differentiator.

**Instagram Edits** includes built-in tools to enhance audio and remove background noise, making it great for cleaning up standard mobile recordings. 

**Lappaa** utilizes a 32-bit float internal processing engine for maximum clarity. Furthermore, it offers **Social Audio Normalization**, allowing you to set a specific LUFS target (from -23 to -14). This ensures your voice or music is mastered to compete perfectly with other content on any platform's feed, ensuring consistent loudness across platforms.

---

## Captions and Accessibility

Both apps understand the importance of making videos readable on mute, but they process them differently.

**Instagram Edits** supports automatic captions that you can customize and style. This allows you to quickly add engaging text overlays to your short-form videos.

**Lappaa** generates captions entirely locally on your device. Lappaa can "burn" these captions directly into the exported video file. This ensures that no matter where you send the file—even natively in a WhatsApp group or a private Signal chat—the recipient can instantly read your words.
